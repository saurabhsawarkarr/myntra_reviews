Identify recurring user behaviors and unmet needs that plausibly influence the journey from product interest/wishlist to purchase. Allow completely new patterns to emerge. Create an emerging behavioral segment only when sufficient records show similar behavior, motivation, or friction.

BUSINESS QUESTION: We are investigating user behaviors, motivations, uncertainties, friction points, workarounds, and decision patterns that may influence the journey from product interest/wishlist to purchase.

2. Separate themes from behaviors. "Quality," "price," "size," and "reviews" are themes. Identify what the user actually does because of that issue.
3. Look for the behavioral chain: Product interest -> Save/Wishlist -> Uncertainty/Blocker -> User action/workaround -> Outcome.
4. Only claim a purchase blocker when evidence supports it. 
5. Separate pre-purchase and post-purchase evidence.
6. Do not confuse mentions with behavioral evidence.
7. Do not infer causality unless explicitly supported by the data. Use "associated with," "potential friction," or "evidence suggests".
8. Every opportunity must be supported by directly relevant evidence.
9. Do not use generic app complaints unless explicitly connected to wishlist/purchase decision behavior.
10. Identify workarounds and outcomes (purchased, delayed, abandoned, alternative purchase, etc).
11. Prefer behavioral evidence over sentiment.
12. Do not force opportunities into predefined categories (Fit, Size, Quality, Price, Trust). If the data reveals a completely different problem, surface it.
13. Quantify only from the actual dataset. Frequency, percentages, counts, and scores must come from classified records. Never invent or estimate numbers.
14. The final opportunity should describe an unmet need, not a feature.
15. Be honest about evidence strength. Classify the evidence strength as one of: Direct, Strong Inference, or Unknown. Only Direct and Strong Inference should contribute to opportunity discovery. Unknown must not be treated as evidence.
16. Evaluate how strongly each opportunity connects to the Wishlist -> Purchase journey and classify the connection as:
   - Direct Wishlist/Purchase Evidence
   - Indirect Purchase-Friction Evidence
   - General Shopping Insight
   - Insufficient Evidence
17. Maintain evidence traceability. Preserve original reviews. Never generate synthetic reviews or examples.
