# Detailed Problem Statement & Project Build Specification

## AI-Powered Discovery Engine for Understanding Wishlist-to-Purchase Drop-Off in Online Fashion Shopping

## 1. Project Overview

In online fashion shopping, users frequently discover products they like and add them to a wishlist or save them for later. However, **product interest does not always convert into a purchase**.

A user may save a product because they genuinely intend to buy it, but later postpone or abandon the purchase due to uncertainty around size, fit, quality, price, styling, reviews, alternatives, or other factors. In other cases, the wishlist may simply function as a bookmarking or comparison tool rather than representing genuine purchase intent.

The core business outcome this project aims to understand is:

> **How can we increase the conversion of users who add fashion products to their wishlist into users who purchase those products within 30 days?**

Before designing any solution, we need to understand the underlying reasons behind this drop-off.

Therefore, this project will build an **AI-Powered Discovery Engine** that collects publicly available user feedback and discussions, processes them at scale, identifies relevant behavioral signals, discovers recurring patterns, and generates evidence-backed opportunity areas.

The initial product focus will be **Myntra**.

---

# 2. Core Problem

Currently, information about users' online fashion shopping behavior is fragmented across multiple public platforms.

Users express their experiences through:

* Google Play Store reviews
* Apple App Store reviews
* Reddit posts and comments
* Other fashion and shopping communities

For the **first version of this project**, we will deliberately limit our data sources to:

```text
1. Google Play Store
2. Apple App Store
3. Reddit
```

Additional sources such as fashion communities, YouTube comments, social media, product reviews, and Q&A will be added only after the initial pipeline is working successfully.

The challenge is not simply to collect reviews.

A traditional review analysis system might produce:

```text
Most common complaint:
Size

Second:
Price

Third:
Delivery
```

This does not answer our actual product problem.

We need to understand:

```text
Why was the user interested?

Did they genuinely intend to purchase?

Why did they save the product?

What uncertainty remained?

What prevented the purchase?

What did they do to resolve the uncertainty?

Did they postpone, abandon,
or purchase an alternative?
```

Therefore, the system must go beyond:

```text
Scraping
+
Sentiment Analysis
+
Review Summarization
```

and instead perform:

```text
Data Collection
        ↓
Cleaning & Normalization
        ↓
Relevance Filtering
        ↓
AI-Powered Information Extraction
        ↓
Signal Aggregation
        ↓
Pattern Discovery
        ↓
Opportunity Identification
        ↓
Opportunity Comparison
```

# 3. Primary Research Question

The central question this project will investigate is:

> **Why do users add or save fashion products but fail to purchase them, and what recurring unmet needs or opportunity areas may influence the conversion from product interest to purchase?**

More specifically, the system should help investigate:

### Wishlist behavior

* Why do users save fashion products?
* Is the wishlist used for genuine purchase intent?
* Is it used for bookmarking?
* Is it used for comparison?
* Is it used to wait for discounts or sales?

### Purchase blockers

* What prevents users from purchasing products they are interested in?
* What causes users to postpone a purchase?
* What uncertainties remain after identifying a product they like?

### Decision-making behavior

* How do users compare multiple products?
* What information do they seek before making a purchase?
* What information do they search for outside the platform?
* What workarounds do they use?

### Potential uncertainty areas

The system will investigate signals related to:

```text
Size
Fit
Quality
Material
Actual appearance
Styling
Price
Discounts
Product reviews
Trust
Alternatives
Comparison
Social validation
```

These should **not be treated as assumptions or predetermined problems**.

They are only areas the discovery engine should be capable of detecting.

For Reddit, the project will use the **Reddit API**, rather than relying on uncontrolled HTML scraping.

```text

Search relevant posts
        ↓
Retrieve relevant post metadata
        ↓
Retrieve comments
        ↓
```

The Reddit API credentials should never be hardcoded.

```text

REDDIT_USER_AGENT
```

## Reddit Data Structure

Posts and comments should preserve their relationship.

Example:

```json
{
  "id": "reddit_post_123",

  "source": "reddit",

  "source_type": "reddit_post",
```

This is important because comments may contain the strongest behavioral insights.

## Objective

From thousands of records, identify conversations that are potentially relevant to our research problem.

We do not need an LLM as the first layer.

The relevance system should use a multi-stage approach.

Identify signals related to:

### Wishlist and saving


### Purchase intent

```text
wanted to buy

```

### Purchase delay

```text
wait

postpone
later
couldn't decide
not buying yet
```

### Uncertainty

```text
confused

couldn't decide
```

### Fashion decision signals

```text
size

style
look
price
discount
sale

```

### External information behavior

```text
YouTube
Instagram
Reddit

```

---

## Layer 2 — Semantic Filtering

Keyword filtering alone will miss relevant statements.

Example:

> "I kept three dresses aside until I figured out which one would suit me."

This may not contain the word "wishlist."

Therefore, use a free local embedding model to identify semantic similarity.

Possible implementation:

```text
Sentence Transformers
```

The system should compare records against semantic research concepts such as:

```text
Saving products for later

Intention to purchase

Difficulty deciding

Purchase uncertainty

Comparing fashion products

Searching outside the shopping platform

Delaying a fashion purchase
```

The output:

```text
data/relevant/relevant_records.json
```

Each record should include:

```text
relevance_score
relevance_reason
```

Example:

```json
{
  "id": "reddit_123",

  "text": "I kept three dresses aside until I figured out which one would suit me.",

  "keyword_score": 0.2,

  "semantic_score": 0.88,

  "final_relevance_score": 0.82
}
```

# PHASE 4 — AI-Powered Information Extraction

## Objective

Use an LLM only after filtering the dataset.

The initial LLM provider will be:

> **Groq API using the available free tier**

The Groq credentials should also be stored securely:

```text
GROQ_API_KEY
```

The API key should not be hardcoded.

---

## Why an LLM is Required

A keyword system can identify words.

For example:

```text
size
review
Instagram
```

But it cannot reliably determine the user's behavioral chain.

Example:

> "I liked the dress, but the reviews were confusing. I wasn't sure about the size, so I checked Instagram reels before deciding."

The LLM should extract:

```text
Motivation:
Liked the dress

Uncertainty:
Size

Problem:
Conflicting or confusing reviews

User Action:
Checked Instagram

External Information:
Instagram reels

Outcome:
Unknown / postponed / purchased
```

---

## Structured Extraction

The LLM must return structured JSON.

Example:

```json
{
  "is_relevant": true,

  "wishlist_behavior": {
    "detected": true,
    "type": "potential_purchase",
    "confidence": 0.85
  },

  "motivation": [
    "liked_design"
  ],

  "uncertainties": [
    "size",
    "fit"
  ],

  "purchase_blockers": [
    "low_fit_confidence"
  ],

  "user_actions": [
    "searched_external_information"
  ],

  "external_information_sources": [
    "Instagram"
  ],

  "outcome": "unknown",

  "evidence_spans": [
    "wasn't sure about the size",
    "checked Instagram reels"
  ]
}
```

---

## Critical AI Rules

The LLM must:

```text
Only extract information explicitly
stated or strongly supported by the text.

Use "unknown" when information
is not available.

Do not invent user motivations.

Do not assume that every saved
product represents purchase intent.

Do not classify sentiment unless
it directly contributes to the research question.
```

This is important for research quality.

# PHASE 7 — Quantify and Compare Opportunity Areas

Each opportunity should be evaluated using five dimensions.

## 1. Frequency

```text
Number of relevant records
supporting opportunity
─────────────────────────
Total relevant records
```

---

## 2. Severity

Estimate how strongly the issue affects behavior.

Example:

```text
1 → Minor inconvenience

2 → Some friction

3 → Requires additional effort

4 → Causes purchase delay

5 → Causes abandonment or
    alternative purchase
```

---

## 3. Workaround Rate

Measure whether users take additional actions because of the problem.

Examples:

```text
Search YouTube

Check Instagram

Ask friends

Compare other websites

Visit offline stores

Order multiple sizes

Purchase alternatives
```

Calculate:

```text
Users who used workaround
──────────────────────────
Users experiencing issue
```

---

## 4. Business Metric Relevance

Estimate the strength of the observed relationship with:

```text
PRODUCT INTEREST
        ↓
WISHLIST / SAVE
        ↓
BLOCKER
        ↓
DELAY OR ABANDONMENT
```

The system should distinguish between:

```text
Weak evidence:
User mentioned price

Stronger evidence:
User wanted product
→ waited because of price
→ did not purchase
```

---

## 5. Evidence Strength

Measure whether the pattern appears:

```text
Single record
        ↓
Multiple records
        ↓
Repeated behavior
        ↓
Across multiple sources
```
